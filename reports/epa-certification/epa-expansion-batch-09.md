# EPA Expansion Batch 09

## Scope

This batch reviews current and recent Kubota and Yanmar 1800 RPM EPA
certification records against official manufacturer generator-engine material.

## Live Database Changes

- Added eight Kubota generator-engine pages:
  - `D1503-M-E4-BG`
  - `D1703-M-E3-BG`
  - `D1803-CR-TI-E4-BG`
  - `V2203-M-E3-BG`
  - `V2403-CR-TI-E4-BG`
  - `V3300-E3-BG`
  - `V3600-T-E3-BG`
  - `V3800DI-T-E3-BG`
- Corrected four existing Kubota Tier 4 pages with official displacement and
  1800 RPM continuous/standby mechanical ratings.
- Corrected Yanmar `3TNV88F-UG6GE` displacement from 1.494 L to 1.642 L and
  set its primary speed to 1800 RPM.
- Corrected Yanmar `4TNV98C-GGE` and `4TNV98CT-GGE` displacement from
  3.119 L to 3.319 L, set 1800 RPM as primary speed, and added Stage V
  alongside Final Tier 4.
- Added the official Kubota BG generator-engine catalog to 12 Kubota pages.
- Added the official Yanmar industrial-engine product guide to three Yanmar
  pages.

## Certification Crosswalk

- Added 15 reviewed Kubota EPA-to-commercial model aliases.
- Added nine reviewed Yanmar EPA-to-commercial model aliases.
- Kubota mappings require agreement on displacement, aspiration, emissions
  tier and Kubota's published 1800 RPM output.
- Yanmar mappings require agreement on displacement, aspiration, emissions
  tier and EPA power node.
