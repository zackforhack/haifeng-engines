# EPA Expansion Batch 23

Date: 2026-07-25

## Scope

Resolved the 13 non-MTU records in the 2016 constant-speed mapped-brand
review tier:

- Added seven exact Hatz pages: `1D81`, `3L43C`, `3M43`, `3M43Z`,
  `4L43C`, `4M43` and `4M43Z`.
- Added three exact Perkins EPA certification configuration pages:
  `3362/1800`, `3366/1800` and `3468/1800`.
- Mapped Yanmar `3NNGA`, `3TNGA` and `3TTGA` to existing TNV commercial
  pages. Their displacement, power, emissions families and 1800 RPM
  operation match the previously reviewed `G`-suffix variants.
- Mapped the combined EPA label `1D81 S/Z/T/U` to the new exact Hatz
  `1D81` page. It was not mapped to the separate `1D81C` model.

## Data Policy

The Perkins EPA designations remain exact database models because no
official commercial-model cross-reference was found. Their `power_kw`
values are EPA-certified mechanical ratings; no electrical ratings were
inferred.
