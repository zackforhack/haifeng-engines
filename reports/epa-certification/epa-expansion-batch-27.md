# EPA Expansion Batch 27

Date: 2026-07-25

## Scope

Closed all nine records in the 2013 constant-speed mapped-brand tier:

- Added six exact John Deere family pages: 3029T, 4024H, 4045H,
  4045T, 5030H and 6090H.
- Added the distinct Isuzu BL-6WG1X Interim Tier 4 configuration.

  configurations.

## Data Policy

The John Deere entries represent EPA family-level model names spanning
multiple detailed generator-drive engine codes. They were not aliased
to a single package page because their certified power ranges cover
multiple commercial configurations.

Isuzu BL-6WG1X remains separate from the existing BQ-6WG1X and 6WG1X
Tier 4 Final pages. EPA mechanical power was not converted into
electrical ratings.
