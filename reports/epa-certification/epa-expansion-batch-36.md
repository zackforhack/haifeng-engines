## New Lion Pages

| Series | Models | EPA certification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LN490 Series | `LN490DZL`, `LN490DZL-1`, `LN490DZL-2`, `LN490DZL-3` | Interim Tier 4 |

All four models share the 2.672 L inline-four platform, 90 x 105 mm bore and
stroke, wastegate turbocharging, air charge cooling, and passive
oxidation-catalyst/DPF aftertreatment. Their EPA-certified 1800 RPM mechanical
outputs range from 20 to 36 kWm for model years 2011 and 2012.

An independent China Chamber of Commerce machinery listing corroborates the
Lion LN490 family, its 2.672 L displacement, four-cylinder water-cooled
configuration, and generating-set application:

- [Lion LN490 product listing](https://www.cccme.cn/products/detail-8117242.aspx)

EPA annual certification data remains the authority for the exact model
suffixes, ratings, aftertreatment and emissions labels.
