# EPA Expansion Batch 25

Date: 2026-07-25

## Scope

Closed all 40 records in the 2015 constant-speed mapped-brand tier:

- Added one exact Komatsu SAA6D170E2-3 page.
- Added ten exact Perkins EPA configuration pages where no verified
  commercial-model cross-reference was available.
- Mapped four Perkins Interim Tier 4 configuration codes to the
  existing 1204E-E44TA and 1204E-E44TTA commercial pages.
- Added MTU 12V4000 P83 and 16V4000 P83 base pages.
- Normalized nine existing MTU Series 1600/4000 pages with their EPA
  displacement, power, RPM and emissions data.
- Mapped 24 MTU application/rating suffixes to commercial base pages.

## Data Policy

EPA `power_kw` values remain certified mechanical ratings. No
electrical output was inferred. Existing MTU electrical and mechanical
rating columns were retained when their base pages were updated.

The 6.598 L and 7.014 L Perkins records remain under their exact EPA
configuration names because the annual family identifiers do not
provide a reliable public commercial-name cross-reference.

## Sources

- EPA Annual Certification Data for nonroad compression-ignition engines.
- Existing official MTU Series 1600 and Series 4000 documents linked to
  the commercial pages.
- Komatsu official product specifications confirming the SAA6D170
  platform's 23.15 L displacement, six-cylinder construction and
  turbocharged/aftercooled design.
