# EPA Expansion Batch 24

Date: 2026-07-25

## Scope

Closed all 37 records in the 2016 MTU Series 2000 constant-speed
mapped-brand tier without creating application-suffix duplicate pages.

- Added 11 missing commercial base pages across the G44, G84, P12,
  P82 and P92R families.
- Normalized five existing G45/G85 pages with EPA mechanical power,
  exact historical displacement, 1800 RPM operation and Tier 2
  certification metadata.
- Mapped 32 reviewed `3A`, `3B`, `3C`, `3D`, `TB` and `TD`
  certification/application names to their commercial base pages.
- Retained five EPA names as directly represented by the new base pages:
  12V2000G84L, 12V/16V2000P12 and 12V/16V2000P92R.

## Integrity Correction

Corrected the historical MTU 18V2000 G85 displacement from 40.2 L to
35.838 L. Its 1191 and 1310 kWm power nodes already matched the EPA
records and MTU's official industrial application reference.

The EPA workbook reports 25.883 L for only `12V2000G85 TB 3B`, while
the other three configurations in the same family report 23.892 L.
The commercial base page retains 23.892 L; the isolated source value is
treated as an annual-certification data anomaly.
