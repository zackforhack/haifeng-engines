# EPA Expansion Batch 35

## Scope

This batch resolves all constant-speed EPA records filed under
`International Motors, LLC`.

- International constant-speed certification records reviewed: **5**
- New exact International generator-engine pages inserted: **5**
- Remaining unmatched International constant-speed records: **0**
- Remaining unmapped-manufacturer constant-speed records: **18**

## New International Pages

| Series | Models | EPA certification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GCB Series | `GCB275`, `GCB300`, `GCB330`, `GCB360`, `GCB390` | Tier 3 |

All five configurations use the 9.354 L inline-six diesel platform at 1800
RPM with turbocharging, air charge cooling and cooled EGR. The EPA data lists
certified mechanical outputs from 254 to 309 kWm for model years 2013 and
2014.

International's official company history confirms that Navistar became
International Motors in 2024. Older California certification orders also
identify GCB generator-set models under the International/Navistar lineage:

- [International announces its Navistar rebrand](https://news.international.com/WeAreInternational)
- [CARB International GCB certification order](https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/sites/default/files/classic/msprog/nvepb/executive_orders/EO%20Web%20Files/OFCI/2003/0003/ofci_ofci_ur-12-60__sdt--20021202.pdf)

EPA annual certification data remains the authority for the 2013–2014
ratings and emissions labels stored in the database.
