# EPA-Certified Expansion Batch 06

Batch 6 adds six missing Cummins commercial engine families and three exact
current generator-drive ratings from the EPA review queue.

## Added

| Model | Displacement | EPA coverage |
|---|---:|---|
| QSB4.5 | 4.46 L | Tier 3 / Tier 4 Final |
| QSK23 | 23.15 L | Tier 2 / Tier 4 Final |
| QSK78 | 77.63 L | Tier 2 / Interim Tier 4 / Tier 4 Final |
| QSK95 | 95.27 L | Tier 2 |
| QST30 | 30.48 L | Interim Tier 4 / Tier 4 Final |
| QSX15 | 14.95 L | Tier 3 / Tier 4 Final |
| QSK38-G17 | 37.89 L | Tier 2 |
| QSK38-G18 | 37.89 L | Tier 2 |
| QSK50-G24 | 50.51 L | Tier 2 |

Cummins' current North America rating card identifies QSK38-G17,
QSK38-G18 and QSK50-G24 in generator sets. Exact electrical ratings are stored
for the two QSK38 Centum models; QSK50-G24 retains mechanical power only because
Cummins uses it across more than one generator-set output.

## Rating Policy

EPA rated power is mechanical engine output and is not converted to kWe or kVA.
Electrical ratings are included only when an official Cummins generator-set
document names the exact engine model.
