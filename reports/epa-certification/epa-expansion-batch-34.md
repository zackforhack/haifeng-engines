## New TYM Pages

| Models | Configuration | EPA certification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `T1700-Gen`, `T2300-Gen` | Naturally aspirated | Interim Tier 4 |
| `T2300T-Gen`, `T2400T-Gen` | Turbocharged | Interim Tier 4 |
| `T3400T-Gen` | Turbocharged and air-aftercooled | Tier 3 |

The TYM models align one-for-one with generator-engine configurations
previously certified under Kukje naming. TYM's official merger announcement
confirms that Kukje Machinery was integrated into TYM in 2022, before these
TYM certifications appeared in model years 2023 and 2024:

- [TYM and Kukje Machinery integration](https://group.tym.world/en/all-latest-news/tym-km-integration-dealership)
- [TYM completes its merger with Kukje Machinery](https://group.tym.world/en/all-latest-news/tym-becomes-south-koreas-number-one-agricultural-machinery-brand)

Separate TYM pages preserve the exact EPA model names and make them
discoverable in catalog search. EPA annual certification data remains the
authority for the mechanical ratings and emissions labels.
