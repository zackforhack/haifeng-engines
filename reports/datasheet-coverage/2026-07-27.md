## Counting Definitions

- **Any document:** At least one engine_pdfs row of any type is linked to the engine.
- **Datasheet:** At least one linked engine_pdfs row is classified as datasheet.
- **Exclusive datasheet:** At least one linked datasheet storage file is linked to only this engine record. Shared family sheets can still be valid but are excluded from this stricter diagnostic.
