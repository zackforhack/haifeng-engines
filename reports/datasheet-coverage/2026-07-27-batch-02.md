links were retained. Generac downloads were assembled from verified HTTP byte
ranges because the manufacturer CDN truncated ordinary full-file responses.
The SG500 file was compressed from 22.3 MB to 2.5 MB before storage.

## Classification Correction

The existing 2024 John Deere *Generator Drive Diesel Engines* technical
selection guide was verified locally. Its 13 pages contain model-specific
engine names, emissions categories, 1500/1800 RPM, prime and standby kWm, kVA,
kWe, generator efficiency, fan power, and dual-frequency availability.

All 25 existing links to covered John Deere model/variant records were
corrected from `brochure` to `datasheet`. No duplicate file or link row was
added. Sixteen engines gained datasheet coverage because nine of the 25 already
had a separate model-specific datasheet.

## Remaining Generac Gaps

The five remaining uncovered Generac records are legacy engine records: 4.2L
V6 Gas, 5.4L V8 Gas, 6.8L V10 Gas, G12.9L G20, and 13.3L Hino Gas. The official
MGG100M operating manual is correctly classified as a manual rather than a
datasheet.
